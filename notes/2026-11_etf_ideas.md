## Quantum computing (added 10 Nov 2026)
The idea: pick a few "random" stocks that could take off in 10 to 20 years. Quantum computing looks like the next big thing.

Two EU ETFs:

| ETF | ISIN | Cost | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| VanEck Quantum Computing (QNTM) | IE0007Y8Y157 | 0.55% | 31 companies. Pure players (IonQ, D-Wave) mixed with giants that research quantum (Microsoft, Amazon, NEC, Hitachi, Fujitsu, Siemens) and big banks. Up about 32% in the year to July 2026 |
| WisdomTree Quantum Computing | IE000W8WMSL2 | 0.50% | Weights companies by how much of their business is quantum |

Pure players, as of mid 2026:
- IonQ: the biggest, about 260 to 270 million dollars of revenue expected in 2026, still losing money
- Rigetti and D-Wave: a few million dollars of revenue a quarter, valued at about 4 billion each
- Quantinuum (Honeywell): listed in June 2026 at over 15 billion dollars, about 500 times its sales

Added to the study: IonQ, Rigetti, D-Wave, Quantinuum and IBM. They will show as SPECULATIVE (no free cash flow).

## Questions for the discussion
- Research on "lottery" stocks (big jumps, small companies, a great story) shows they do worse on average, because people overpay for the chance of a jackpot. Is quantum different?
- The technology can win and the stocks still lose: the internet won, most 2000 internet stocks died. Who wins in quantum may be a company that doesn't exist yet, or a giant I already hold in VWCE
- If I do it: a quantum ETF instead of picking, and a small amount (1 to 2% of the portfolio) that I can lose without pain
