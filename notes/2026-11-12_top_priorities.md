# 12 Nov 2026: my top priorities

These are the companies I want to own one day because I love what they do or because almost every good investor admires them: Micron, Microsoft, Alphabet, Apple, Amazon, SpaceX, Meta, IBM, Netflix, Costco, Berkshire Hathaway, Ferrari, Hermès, Visa and Tesla. They sit in fair_inputs.csv with list = priority.

## The rule
- At or below my fair price: START PLAN. A monthly plan starts. No other stock has to leave: the money comes from VWCE, which can go down to 50%. Up to 4 priorities can have a plan on top of my 10 stocks.
- 15% or more below my fair price: BUY BIG. On top of the plan, a one-off buy from the extra money (up to 200 € a month, buys of 100 to 200 €).
- Within 5% of the fair price: PRIORITY CLOSE, so I am ready.
- Above the fair price: WAIT. I love the company, but I do not overpay.

## Two special cases
- Micron: memory is a boom and bust business, so I use a normal year's profit (70 USD a share, close to what Goldman Sachs uses), not this year's record. The analysts' number is not compared.
- SpaceX: the profit a share is small next to the price (about 1.3 USD), so its fair price depends a lot on the growth I pick. Treat it as a rough guide.

## Sectors when one comes in
- Microsoft, Alphabet, Apple, Amazon, Meta, IBM: sector Big tech, ai = no. They have many businesses, AI is one of them. The 20% sector limit still applies, so at most 4 of them at 5%.
- Micron: sector Chips and AI, ai = yes. That sector is at 19% today, so Micron would need a smaller weight or a smaller plan in another chip stock.
- SpaceX: sector Space, ai = no.
- Ferrari and Hermès: priced in euros, so they also balance my dollar exposure. Sector Luxury.
- Visa: very close to Mastercard, which I already own. Owning both is mostly the same bet.
- Tesla: on my numbers the price is many times my fair price. It stays here so I see the gap, not because a buy is likely.
- Berkshire and Hermès: their profit is not compared with the analysts (Berkshire's profit jumps with its investments, Yahoo has no number for Hermès).
