# 12 Nov 2026: my top priorities

These are the 9 companies I want to own one day because I love what they do: Micron, Microsoft, Alphabet, Apple, Amazon, SpaceX, Meta, IBM and Intel (added 12 Nov, 23h). They sit in fair_inputs.csv with list = priority. Netflix, Costco, Berkshire Hathaway, Ferrari, Hermès, Visa and Tesla moved to the watchlist the same day: 15 was too many to follow well. How each fair price was built is in 2026-11-12_fair_values.md.

## The rule
- 10% or more below my fair price, with at least two independent checks agreeing: START PLAN. A monthly plan starts. No other stock has to leave: the money comes from VWCE, which can go down to 50%. Up to 4 priorities can have a plan on top of my 10 stocks.
- 20% or more below my fair price, same checks: BUY BIG. On top of the plan, a one-off buy from the extra money (up to 200 € a month, buys of 100 to 200 €).
- Within 5% of the start price: PRIORITY CLOSE, so I am ready.
- Above the fair price: WAIT. I love the company, but I do not overpay.

## Two special cases
- Micron: memory is a boom and bust business, so I use a normal year's profit (65 USD a share, close to Goldman Sachs' 70), not this year's record. The analysts' number is not compared.
- SpaceX: the profit a share is small next to the price (2.25 USD expected in 2027), so its fair price comes from three scenarios and leans on Morningstar. Treat it as a rough guide.

## Sectors when one comes in
- Microsoft, Alphabet, Apple, Amazon, Meta, IBM: sector Big tech, ai = no. They have many businesses, AI is one of them. The 20% sector limit still applies, so at most 4 of them at 5%.
- Micron: sector Chips and AI, ai = yes. My AI limit (30%) is already full (Nvidia, TSMC, ASML, Broadcom, Vertiv, GE Vernova, Vistra), so Micron needs another AI stock to shrink first.
- SpaceX: sector Space, ai = no.
- Intel: sector Chips and AI, ai = yes. A turnaround: fair price from DCF 35%, Morningstar 35%, banks 30%, no past P/E (it lost money), discount 11%. My DCF alone says much less than Morningstar, so treat it as very uncertain.
