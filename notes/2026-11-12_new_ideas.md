# Six AI ideas (12 Nov 2026)

I looked at Marvell, IREN, Corning, Lumentum, Bloom Energy and Coherent. None comes in today. Two go on the watchlist.

| Company | What it does | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Marvell | Custom AI chips and data center connectivity | Watchlist, entry 149 dollars |
| Corning | Optical fiber and specialty glass | Watchlist, entry 103 dollars |
| Lumentum | Lasers and optical parts | No: Marvell is buying it, so its price now follows Marvell |
| IREN | Rents out GPUs in its own data centers | No |
| Bloom Energy | Fuel cells that power data centers | No: price far above my fair price |
| Coherent | Optical transceivers and lasers | No: burns cash, pays nothing to shareholders |

## Why
- Marvell: revenue up 37% in Q2 FY2027 and guided to about 12 billion dollars this year and 18 billion next year. But it is number two in custom AI chips behind Broadcom, which I already own, and the all stock deal for Lumentum gives Lumentum holders a third of the company. That is a lot of new shares, which breaks my rule on dilution. About 13% above my fair price
- Corning: sales up 19% and earnings per share up 36% in Q3 2026, with optical sales up 44% and long deals with Verizon, AT&T and Nvidia. A real leader, but fiber has strong rivals and the price is about 17% above my fair price
- Lumentum: great quarter (revenue up 136%) and capacity sold out, but each share becomes 4.5 Marvell shares when the deal closes in 2028
- IREN: AI cloud revenue almost tripled, but it still loses money, has about 6.7 billion dollars of convertible debt, issued many new shares and competes with many other GPU clouds. It fails my rules on competition and on dilution
- Bloom Energy: revenue up 138% in Q3 2026, but free cash flow was negative, two customers made 69% of revenue, the share count grew about 5.5% in nine months and it competes with GE Vernova turbines, which I already own. My fair price is about 107 dollars against about 205 dollars
- Coherent: revenue up 50% and data center sales up 85%, with a 5 year deal of about 20 billion dollars. But free cash flow is negative, there are no dividends or buybacks and many strong rivals make the same parts. My fair price is about 168 dollars against about 210 dollars

## Sector limit
All six depend on AI data centers. My stocks in Chips and AI are 15% of the portfolio and the limit is 20%, so at most one of them could come in.
