## 1. What does it sell and to whom?
Software for creative work (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere), documents (Acrobat, PDF) and marketing for companies. Almost all revenue comes from subscriptions.

Q3 FY2026: revenue +13% to 6.76 bn USD. AI recurring revenue +150% to 650 M USD. New recurring revenue fell 39%. FY2026 guidance: revenue 26.6 bn USD.

## 3. Why could it be important in the future?
Adobe is putting AI inside its own tools (Firefly) and offers free versions to bring in new users (over 100 million monthly). If it stays the standard for professional creative work and PDF, AI makes its tools more useful, not less.

## 4. Moat
- Industry standard: designers, agencies and companies learn and share files in Adobe formats
- Acrobat and PDF are used by almost every company
- Very high margins and cash flow

## 5. What does the market know that I don't? (risks)
- Generative AI does exactly what Adobe's tools do: Canva, Midjourney and Runway let anyone create good images and video
- New recurring revenue fell 39%, which could mean fewer new customers
- Stock-based pay of about 1.9 bn USD a year dilutes shareholders (I removed it from free cash flow)
- Same theme as Wolters Kluwer: if AI replaces professional software, both fall
- Share price down about 30% in 2026
