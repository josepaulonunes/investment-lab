## 1. What does it sell and to whom?
The world's biggest animal health company. Medicines and vaccines for pets (Apoquel and Cytopoint for allergies, Librela for arthritis pain, Simparica for fleas and ticks) and for farm animals. It sells mostly to vets and farmers.

## 3. Why could it be important in the future?
People keep spending more on their pets, treating them like family. Pet medicine is mostly paid in cash, not by insurers or governments, so prices are less regulated than in human health.

## 4. Moat
- Biggest sales force and relationships with vets around the world
- Strong pipeline and decades of R&D in animal health
- Products approved by regulators, which takes years for any competitor

## 5. What does the market know that I don't? (risks)
- Apoquel faces cheaper competition: dermatology sales fell 11% in Q1 2026
- Safety worries about Librela; Librela and Solensia sales fell 8%
- Fewer vet visits and pet owners more sensitive to price
- Revenue growth passes my 5% rule only just (5.0% a year)
- Debt went up in 2025 to pay for buybacks
- Share price down about 38% in 2026 and about 70% from its peak
