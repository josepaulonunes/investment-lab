# ASML (ASML, Euronext Amsterdam)

Date: 2026-10-28
Status: analysed, not buying yet (watch list)
Buy zone: about 1,050 to 1,100 EUR (central fair value 1,274 EUR minus 15 to 20%)

## 1. What does it sell and to whom?
The machines that print chips (lithography). It is the only company in the world that makes EUV machines, which are needed for the most advanced chips. Clients: TSMC, Samsung, Intel and memory makers.

## 2. Why could it be important in the future?
Every advanced AI chip goes through an ASML machine. Q3 2026 was a record (11.7 bn EUR sales, 57.5% gross margin, 5.0 bn EUR net income). 2026 sales expected at 44 to 45 bn EUR, and supply for 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet demand.

## 3. Moat
A real monopoly in EUV: decades of R&D, a supply chain nobody can copy (Zeiss optics) and a huge installed base that pays for service.

## 4. Risks
- Chips are cyclical: orders can fall fast when clients cut investment
- Few clients: TSMC alone is a big share of sales
- Geopolitics: export limits to China
- Price: the market already pays for a lot of growth

## 5. Key ratios (2021 to 2025, from analyse_company.py)
- EBITDA margin about 34 to 38%, FCF margin about 34% in 2025 (Prysmian: 6%)
- ROIC 59 to 80% (Prysmian: 15%)
- Net cash about 8.6 bn EUR (no net debt)
- FCF swings a lot (3.3 bn EUR in 2023, 11.1 bn EUR in 2025) because clients pay in advance for machines
