## 1. What does it sell and to whom?
Software and expert information for professionals who cannot afford to be wrong: doctors and nurses (UpToDate), accountants and tax advisers (CCH), lawyers and compliance teams. Most clients pay a yearly subscription and use the tools every day.

2026 outlook: adjusted operating margin about 28%, free cash flow 1.30 to 1.35 bn EUR, buybacks up to 500 M EUR.

## 3. Why could it be important in the future?
Rules in tax, health and law keep getting more complex, and professionals need answers they can trust and defend. About 70% of digital revenue already comes from AI-powered products, so the company is using AI inside its own tools.

## 4. Moat
- Content built and checked by experts over decades, which clients need to be able to trust and cite
- Software built into daily workflows, so switching is slow and risky
- 83% recurring revenue

## 5. What does the market know that I don't? (risks)
- AI could let clients get good enough answers much cheaper, from general models or AI-native startups in legal and medical search
- Pricing per user could suffer if clients need fewer people
- Debt went up to pay for buybacks: equity is now only 0.8 bn EUR and net debt is 2.0x EBITDA
- The share price fell about 67% from its peak
