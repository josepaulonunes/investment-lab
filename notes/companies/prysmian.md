# Prysmian (PRY, Borsa Italiana)

Date: 2026-10-28
Status: analysed, not buying yet (watch list)
Buy zone: 93 to 99 EUR (central fair value 116 EUR minus 15 to 20%)

## 1. What does it sell and to whom?
The world's biggest maker of energy and telecom cables. It sells high voltage and subsea cables to grid operators and offshore wind developers (Transmission), medium and low voltage cables to utilities (Power Grid), cables and systems for buildings, industry and data centres (Electrification) and optical fibre (Digital Solutions).

## 3. Why could it be important in the future?
Electrification, offshore wind, grid upgrades and AI data centres all need much more cable. Grids are now a bottleneck for the energy transition and for AI, and Prysmian is the biggest player where it matters most (high voltage and subsea).

## 4. What makes it hard to copy? (moat)
- Scale and know how in high voltage and subsea cables, where only a few companies in the world can deliver
- Own cable laying vessels and factories, which take years and billions to build
- A backlog of about 17 bn EUR that gives years of visibility

## 5. What could go wrong? (risks)
- Atkore acquisition (closing expected end of 2026): EV about 3.3 bn EUR, 9.8x EBITDA (7.1x with synergies), paid with debt and treasury shares. Electrification (most cyclical, lower margins) gets bigger and leverage goes up after closing. The H1 2026 rise in net debt (3.1 to 4.1 bn EUR) is not Atkore yet.
- Lower multiple: the market now pays less for each euro of profit. Jefferies cut its valuation from 16x to 14x EV/EBITDA even after raising estimates (Sep 2026). The share price fell about 35% from the 2026 peak.
- Data centre slowdown: the CEO said a possible slowdown in data centre construction is a market concern.
- Copper and metal prices: revenue moves with copper, so reported margins can look different from real margins.
- Project risk in Transmission: big subsea projects can have delays and cost overruns.

## 7. First impression
A great business in the right place: margins went up every year (7.7% to 12.2%), profit turns into cash and debt goes down fast after each acquisition. The question is not quality, it is price and the cycle.

## 8. Notes on the accounts
- 2024: net debt jumped to 4.3 bn EUR because of the Encore Wire acquisition in the US (about 4.1 bn EUR, together with Warren & Brown). It went back down to 3.1 bn EUR in one year.
- 2025: net income of 1,270 M EUR includes a one-off gain of 346 M EUR from selling the YOFC stake. Without it, normal net income was about 924 M EUR. This is also why reported EBITDA and EBIT in 2025 are higher than the adjusted figures.
- Source: Prysmian annual results press releases 2021 to 2025 (prysmian.com, Investors, Results Centre). Checked revenue, adjusted EBITDA and net debt for 2025 against the press release.

## Verdict (v1)
Fairly valued, close to the central scenario. The price asks for modest growth compared with history, so the risk is more about the cycle turning than about paying too much. Margin of safety below 20%, so by my rule: not a buy yet. Watch Q3 results (November) and the Atkore closing.
