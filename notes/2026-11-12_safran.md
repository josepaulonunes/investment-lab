## Why Safran
- It makes the LEAP engine with GE for most A320neo and 737 MAX jets, and earns on the service of each engine for 30 years or more
- Revenue up about 15% in 2026, free cash flow of 4.7 to 4.9 billion euros, margins near 18%
- Some defense too (Rafale engines, navigation, optronics, guided bombs, missile engines), so it gains from Europe rearming without depending on it
- It is in euros, the only stock that is not American or Asian

## Risks
- Spare parts are growing slower than before
- It needs Airbus and Boeing to keep raising production
- Tariffs between Europe and the US
