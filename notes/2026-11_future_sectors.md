# Sectors for the next 15 to 20 years (10 Nov 2026)

To discuss, beyond AI and quantum. A sector that grows does not mean its stocks do well: the money goes to the companies with little competition, the picks and shovels.

| Sector | Why it could grow | How early | Companies in the study |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy infrastructure and nuclear | Power demand rising again, old grids, AI data centers | Theme for 2 to 3 years | GE Vernova, Siemens Energy, Schneider, Eaton, Prysmian, Quanta, Cameco, BWXT, Constellation, Vistra |
| Longevity and genomic medicine | Ageing, gene editing, personalised medicine | Early | Eli Lilly, Vertex, Regeneron, Thermo Fisher, Danaher, Illumina, CRISPR Therapeutics, Intellia |
| Robotics and automation | Labour shortages, ageing | Industrial is mature | Intuitive Surgical, Keyence, SMC, Fanuc, ABB, Siemens, Rockwell |
| Humanoid robots | Robots that work like people | Very early | Tesla, UBTech, and the parts makers Harmonic Drive (precision gears) and Nabtesco |
| Photonics and optical computing | AI needs light based links inside and between data centers | Early | Broadcom, Coherent, Lumentum, Corning, Ciena |
| Cybersecurity | More attacks, critical infrastructure | Mainstream | CrowdStrike, Palo Alto, Fortinet |
| Critical minerals | Copper, lithium, rare earths | Copper known, rare earths rising | Materials ETF (BHP, Rio Tinto, Freeport) |
| Water and precision farming | Water scarcity, food security | Slow and steady | Veolia, Waste Management |
| Space infrastructure | Satellite internet, Earth observation, defence | Early, with hype | Rocket Lab, AST SpaceMobile, Iridium, MDA Space |
| Batteries | Electric cars, grid storage | Middle stage, margins falling | Tesla, BYD |
| Nuclear fusion | Almost unlimited energy, if it works | Very early, maybe 2035 to 2045 | No listed pure player. Leaders (Commonwealth Fusion, Helion) are private. Watch only |

## My ranking for 15 to 20 years
1. Energy infrastructure and nuclear
2. Longevity and genomics, through the big companies and the tool makers
3. Photonics and advanced chips
4. Robotics and automation, humanoids through parts makers
5. Cybersecurity, minerals and water: good themes, harder to profit from
6. Space, batteries and fusion: small amounts only, or watch

## Rule for early themes
Small amounts, spread out (ETF or several companies), lots of patience. Earlier means more companies that fail.
