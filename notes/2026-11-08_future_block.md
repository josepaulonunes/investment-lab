# Plan change (8 Nov 2026)

From 85/10/5 at 400 EUR a month to a 7 ETF portfolio at 700 EUR a month.

## What changed
- Monthly amount: 400 to 700 EUR
- New future block (12%): WTAI 4%, GRID 4%, NUKL 2%, health care 2%
- Core down from 85% to 75%, gold down from 5% to 3%
- First purchase with the new mix on 2 Dec 2026

## Why
- I believe AI, electrification, nuclear and health care will shape the next decades
- VWCE already holds most of these companies (about a third of it is tech), so the extra bet stays small
- One ETF per sector so I own a bit of each link: chips and software (WTAI), power grid and data center power (GRID), uranium and reactors (NUKL), medicines and medical tech (health care)
- Health care came from gold: both are defensive, but health care companies grow and pay their way

## What I looked at and left out
- QDVE and Nasdaq 100: more of the same giants I already hold in VWCE
- VVSM: only 25 chip makers, too concentrated
- Emerging markets and value ETFs: their top holdings are also AI chip makers
- More themes (robotics, batteries, space, defence): already inside VWCE, and 1% each changes nothing

## What I accept
- Thematic ETFs often do worse than the market after they become popular. That is why the block is capped
- NUKL is the most speculative: uranium prices are cyclical and some holdings have no revenue yet
- Any of these can lag VWCE for years. I don't sell them for that
