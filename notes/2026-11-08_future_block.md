# Plan change (8 and 9 Nov 2026)

From 85/10/5 to a 7 product portfolio, and a bigger monthly amount.

## What changed
- Monthly amount: up by about 75%
- New future block (13%): GRID 4%, health care 4%, materials 3%, NUKL 2%
- Core down from 85% to 74%, gold down from 5% to 3%
- First purchase with the new mix on 2 Dec 2026

## Why
- I believe electrification, health care and nuclear will shape the next decades
- Each future ETF has a job. GRID is my strongest conviction. Health care is the calm part (it fell about 39% in 2008 against 58% for the market). Materials is the cheap part (forward P/E about 15 against about 17 for MSCI World) and holds the copper and metals that electrification needs. Nuclear is a small bet: it is barely inside VWCE, and dozens of countries are building reactors
- GRID stays at 4% because it is the most expensive (P/E about 30)
- Materials instead of consumer staples or financials: staples are more expensive than the market and matched it since 2000, and financials are already about 15% of the portfolio through VWCE

## Why I dropped the AI ETF (WTAI)
- Its largest holdings (NVIDIA, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta) are also the largest in VWCE, and about a third of VWCE is already tech
- What it adds is only more weight in mid size AI companies, about 0.05% of my portfolio each
- It rose about 47% in a year and costs 0.40%. After its last big run it fell 42% in 2022
- If AI wins, VWCE wins too. The 2% went to VWCE, not to another theme: a limit is not a target

## What I looked at and left out
- QDVE, Nasdaq 100, semiconductors: more of the same giants I already hold
- Emerging markets and value ETFs: their top holdings are also AI chip makers
- Consumer staples, financials, robotics, space, defence: already inside VWCE, more expensive, or pure hype

## What I accept
- Thematic ETFs often do worse than the market after they become popular, and many close. That is why the block is small
- Materials is cyclical: it fell about 67% in 2008. About 17% of it is gold miners
- NUKL is the most speculative: uranium prices are cyclical and some holdings have no revenue yet
- Any of these can lag VWCE for years. I don't sell them for that
- This is a conviction choice, not a proven way to beat the market
