# Plan change (8 Nov 2026)

From 85/10/5 to an 8 product portfolio, and a bigger monthly amount.

## What changed
- Monthly amount: up by about 75%
- New future block (15%): GRID 4%, health care 4%, materials 3%, WTAI 2%, NUKL 2%
- Core down from 85% to 72%, gold down from 5% to 3%
- First purchase with the new mix on 2 Dec 2026

## Why
- I believe electrification, health care, AI and nuclear will shape the next decades
- Each future ETF has a job. GRID is my strongest conviction. Health care is the calm part (it fell about 39% in 2008 against 58% for the market). Materials is the cheap part (forward P/E about 15 against about 17 for MSCI World) and holds the copper and metals that electrification needs. AI and nuclear are small bets
- GRID stays at 4% because it is the most expensive (P/E about 30)
- AI only 2%: about a third of VWCE is already tech, and WTAI rose about 47% in the last year
- Materials instead of consumer staples or financials: staples are more expensive than the market and matched it since 2000, and financials are already about 17% of VWCE
- Target 15% with a band of 12 to 18%, so a good year for the themes does not force me to change anything

## What I looked at and left out
- QDVE and Nasdaq 100: more of the same giants I already hold in VWCE
- VVSM: only 25 chip makers, too concentrated
- Emerging markets and value ETFs: their top holdings are also AI chip makers
- Consumer staples, financials, utilities: already inside VWCE or GRID
- More themes (robotics, batteries, space, defence): already inside VWCE, and 1% each changes nothing

## What I accept
- Thematic ETFs often do worse than the market after they become popular. That is why the block is capped
- Materials is cyclical: it fell about 67% in 2008. About 17% of it is gold miners, so my gold exposure is a bit above 3%
- NUKL is the most speculative: uranium prices are cyclical and some holdings have no revenue yet
- Any of these can lag VWCE for years. I don't sell them for that
- This is a conviction choice, not a proven way to beat the market
