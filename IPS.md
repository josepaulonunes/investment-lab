# Investment Policy Statement

My rules, written before investing. I only change them in the January review.

- Emergency fund and short term savings stay in cash, never in the portfolio
- Investing: a fixed amount every month with automatic plans on the 2nd. More when my salary goes up

## Why this mix
- VWCE holds about 3,800 large and mid companies from about 50 countries. It is the engine and already holds the big names in tech, health and energy
- IUSN adds about 3,600 small companies that VWCE barely holds
- The future block is my conviction bet on the sectors I think will shape the next decades: AI (chips, memory, networks), power grid and electrification, nuclear, and health care. It is kept small on purpose
- Gold is insurance for crises and high inflation. It earns nothing on its own, so 3% is enough
- No bonds for now. My cash savings already play that role

## Rules
1. Invest every month, whatever the market does.
2. Never sell because prices fall. A 40% drop will happen at some point. I keep buying.
3. Cash never goes below the emergency fund plus short term savings.
4. Review only once a year, in January. If a block is outside its band, fix it with new money, not by selling.
5. Never sell IUSN or a future ETF just because it lags VWCE. That can last years.
6. The future block never goes above 15%. If it does, new money goes elsewhere until it is back on target.
7. Maximum 7 ETFs. A new one only comes in if another one goes out.
8. No individual stocks for now. The future study in future/ is a test: in January 2028 I compare its picks with the future ETFs.
9. No changes because of videos, news or social media posts. New ideas wait for January.
10. If I need money for studies or a move: pause plans if needed, never sell.
11. If I move country, check how the new country taxes ETFs before doing anything.

## Tax notes (Portugal)
- Accumulating ETFs: no tax until I sell
- On sale: 28% on the gain, with 10%, 20% or 30% of the gain excluded after 2, 5 or 8 years
- The gold product is an ETC, not a fund. Check its tax treatment before selling
