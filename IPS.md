# Investment Policy Statement

My rules, written before investing. I only change them in the January review.

## Money
- Emergency fund and short term savings stay in cash, never in the portfolio
- Investing: 400 EUR per month with automatic plans on the 2nd. More when my salary goes up

## Targets
| Block | Target | Band | What | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core | 85% | 82 to 88% | Vanguard FTSE All-World (VWCE), IE00BK5BQT80 | 340 EUR |
| Small | 10% | 7 to 13% | iShares MSCI World Small Cap (IUSN), IE00BF4RFH31 | 40 EUR |
| Gold | 5% | 3 to 7% | Invesco Physical Gold, IE00B579F325 | 20 EUR |

## Why this mix
- VWCE holds about 3,800 large and mid companies from about 50 countries. It is the engine
- IUSN adds about 3,600 small companies that VWCE barely holds, so it lowers my dependence on a few giant tech names
- Gold is insurance for crises and high inflation. It earns nothing on its own, so 5% is enough
- No bonds for now. My cash savings already play that role

## Rules
1. Invest every month, whatever the market does.
2. Never sell because prices fall. A 40% drop will happen at some point. I keep buying.
3. Cash never goes below the emergency fund plus short term savings.
4. Review only once a year, in January. If a block is outside its band, fix it with new money, not by selling.
5. Never sell IUSN just because it lags VWCE. That can last years.
6. No individual stocks and no thematic ETFs. I tested both and the evidence is against them for me.
7. No changes because of videos, news or social media posts. New ideas wait for January.
8. If I need money for studies or a move: pause plans if needed, never sell.
9. If I move country, check how the new country taxes ETFs before doing anything.

## Tax notes (Portugal)
- Accumulating ETFs: no tax until I sell
- On sale: 28% on the gain, with 10%, 20% or 30% of the gain excluded after 2, 5 or 8 years
- The gold product is an ETC, not a fund. Check its tax treatment before selling
