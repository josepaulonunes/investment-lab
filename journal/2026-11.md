# November 2026

## What changed
- I rebuilt the whole method in one Excel model: one DCF per company, a sector beta, three scenarios, comparables and revenue by business for Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon.
- This repo now only shows the results. The old screener and the daily alerts are in the tag `v1-old`.
- How strict I am with each company now comes from how uncertain its value is, not from my gut.
- Core companies get the full monthly buy up to 10% above fair value and half up to 20%. Before it was 30%, which meant buying some of them with an expected return below VWCE.
- The cash reserve has a cap. Above it, the money goes to VWCE.

- Paused: TSMC (36% above fair value) and ASML (27% above). They come back on their own when the price falls.
